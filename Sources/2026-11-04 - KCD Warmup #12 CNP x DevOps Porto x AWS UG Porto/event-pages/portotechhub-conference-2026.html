More Than Just a
Conference, It's
a Meeting Point.

Porto Tech Hub Conference returns this year, bringing together the tech community for another edition of knowledge sharing, meaningful connections, and forward-thinking discussions.

Taking place on November 10th, at Alfândega do Porto, the conference continues to grow as a reference event in the Portuguese tech ecosystem. Over the years, it has welcomed more than 10k participants, gathering professionals, companies, and industry leaders.

At its core, the Porto Tech Hub Conference is about bridging perspectives, connecting talent, industry, and innovation. From software development to product, business, and beyond, the event creates a space where experiences are shared, challenges are discussed, and new opportunities emerge.

More than just a conference, it's a meeting point for those who are building what comes next.
