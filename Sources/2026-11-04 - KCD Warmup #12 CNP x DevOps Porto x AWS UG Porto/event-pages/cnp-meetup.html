About us

🚀 Welcome to Cloud Native Porto 🌆

🌐 Cloud Native Technologies make the world run as they enable organizations to build, run and scale applications suited for all cloud environments. With the creation of the chapter for Cloud Native Porto, we aim to provide our community of developers, engineers, architects, and tech enthusiasts with a place were they can get together and share their passion and knowledge for building scalable, resilient, and modern software systems.

🏛️ Rooted in the dynamic city of Porto, we have as our mission to foster collaboration, knowledge sharing, and innovation around tools and practices like Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD, Observability, and the broader Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) ecosystem.

Whether you're just starting your cloud-native journey or are a seasoned expert, Cloud Native Porto offers a welcoming and inclusive space for learning, networking, and inspiration for all. So the missing piece now is you, to help us grow this community even further.

📍Join us for meetups, workshops, and discussions as we explore cutting-edge technologies, share real-world experiences, and grow together in the ever-evolving cloud-native landscape. You can find us in[ https://community.cncf.io/cloud-native-porto/](https://community.cncf.io/cloud-native-porto/)

🗨️ Check our slack channel for updates or if you just want a place to hangout, all welcomed https://cloud-native.slack.com/archives/C088H34U0TF. Also don't forget to follow us on LinkedIn at https://www.linkedin.com/company/cloud-native-porto/.

🗣️ Want to share your Cloud Native Experience with the community, we are always looking for speakers, both in person and remote. Please apply via https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdKIpo2gUiTgUz9GXdoQgtEwhT_EZZHFsy7yz56tEAFTgmyZQ/viewform

👉 Meetup after meetup, made possible by our long-standing sponsor, synvert xgeeks. They fund our Meetup Pro subscription, among other things, which is what allows this group to exist in the first place. synvert xgeeks, a GlobalLogic company, accelerates enterprise success with AI and Cloud Native development, turning the promise of DevOps, data, and digital solutions into real business impact for the world's leading brands, now backed by GlobalLogic's global engineering scale.

  • KCD Warmup Meetup - #12 CNP x DevOps Porto x AWS UG Porto

    Wed, Nov 4 · 6:30 PM WEST
    Tech Hub (Sonae), Av. de Dom Mendo 4470, Maia, Portugal, Maia, PT

    🚀 Three communities, one night, and the countdown to KCD Porto starts here.

    Cloud Native Porto is joining forces with DevOps Porto and AWS User Group Porto for a special KCD Warmup edition, hosted by MC Digital at the Sonae Tech Hub. Two practitioner talks: what cloud native really means inside a large organisation, and how to use the cloud itself to keep the cloud secure.

    Consider it a warm-up for KCD Porto × DevOpsDays Portugal, happening on 19–20 November at Super Bock Arena.

    📍 Venue: Tech Hub (Sonae), Porto
    📅 Date: November 4, 2026
    ⏰ Doors open: 18:30

    ***

    🗓 Agenda
    18:30 · Check-in & Networking

    19:00 · Welcome & Intros

    19:10 · What “Cloud Native” actually means for a service at MC Digital, and the golden rules and paved road we are building to get there

    🎤 Victor Hugo Silva, Principal Technical Architect @ MC Digital

    At MC Digital, “cloud native” isn’t a deployment location. It’s an architecture built for dynamic, distributed environments. It requires stateless instances, routine failure handling, zero-drama scaling, lightning-fast GitOps rollouts, and deep end-to-end observability without debuggers.

    We’ll dive into our Cloud-Native Guidelines for Software Development and break down our Golden Rules for backend services, covering foundations, non-negotiable standards, deliberate stances and the paved road.

    19:45 · Governing the Ungovernable: Leveraging the Cloud to Secure the Cloud

    🎤 Chris Farris, AWS Security Hero & Owner @ PrimeHarbor Technologies

    The cloud has democratised IT, taking it from the hands of sysadmin gatekeepers and allowing anyone in your organisation to create and deploy applications, often without the security controls you’d want. With the fall of the IT Empire, how does cybersecurity protect our assets from the roving bands of developers and DevOps, who expose things to the internet in new and unexpected ways?

    Cloud providers offer us some tools, but they fall short of actual solutions. In this session, Chris will walk you through the concept of Security Invariants, how they can be applied to your organisation, and how we can leverage event-driven architecture to cover the edge cases the cloud providers don’t account for. Whether you’re a large, well-funded enterprise or a small, scrappy start-up, you’ll get some actionable ideas (and even a bit of code) you can use to govern your cloud estate better.
    20:20 · Food, drinks & networking 🍕🍻

    🤝 Sponsors: MC Digital · synvert xgeeks

    🌐 Community Partners: DevOps Porto · AWS User Group Porto

    A huge thanks to MC Digital for hosting and supporting this edition, to synvert xgeeks for the ongoing support, and to DevOps Porto and AWS UG Porto for making this a three-community night.

    Spots are limited, so grab yours early. See you on November 4th!
