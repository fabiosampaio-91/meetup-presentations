About us

🚀 Welcome to AWS User Group Porto! – Your community for AWS & Cloud Technology in Porto!

Join AWS UG Porto and dive into the world of cloud computing, DevOps, AI/ML, serverless, security, and beyond! Whether you're an experienced cloud architect, a developer exploring AWS services, or just getting started on your cloud journey, AWS UG Porto is the place to learn, share, and grow together.

🌍 What to expect?
🔹 Engaging meetups, workshops, and hands-on sessions on AWS best practices, new services, and real-world applications.

🔹 Learn from experts and industry leaders who will share insights on cloud architecture, scalability, cost optimization, and security.

🔹 Connect with like-minded professionals, AWS Certified Engineers, and cloud enthusiasts to exchange ideas and collaborate.

💡 Why join AWS UG Porto?

AWS UG Porto is all about learning, sharing, and building together. From deep dives into Kubernetes on AWS (EKS) to serverless with AWS Lambda, from data engineering on AWS to AI/ML with SageMaker, this is your chance to stay ahead in the ever-evolving cloud landscape.

  • KCD Warmup Meetup - #8 AWS UG Porto x DevOps Porto x CNP

    Wed, Nov 4 · 6:30 PM WEST
    Tech Hub (Sonae), Av. de Dom Mendo 4470, Maia, Portugal, Maia, PT

    ☁️ AWS User Group Porto is back, and this time we’re bringing two communities along.

    We’re teaming up with DevOps Porto and Cloud Native Porto for a special KCD Warmup edition, hosted by MC Digital at the Sonae Tech Hub. On stage: AWS Security Hero Chris Farris on securing the cloud with the cloud, and MC Digital’s Victor Hugo Silva on the golden rules behind their cloud native services.

    It’s also a warm-up for KCD Porto × DevOpsDays Portugal, on 19–20 November at Super Bock Arena.

    📍 Venue: Tech Hub (Sonae), Porto
    📅 Date: November 4, 2026
    ⏰ Doors open: 18:30

    ***

    🗓 Agenda

    18:30 · Check-in & Networking

    19:00 · Welcome & Intros

    19:10 · What “Cloud Native” actually means for a service at MC Digital, and the golden rules and paved road we are building to get there
    🎤     Victor Hugo Silva, Principal Technical Architect @ MC Digital

    At MC Digital, “cloud native” isn’t a deployment location. It’s an architecture built for dynamic, distributed environments. It requires stateless instances, routine failure handling, zero-drama scaling, lightning-fast GitOps rollouts, and deep end-to-end observability without debuggers.

    We’ll dive into our Cloud-Native Guidelines for Software Development and break down our Golden Rules for backend services, covering foundations, non-negotiable standards, deliberate stances and the paved road.

    19:45 · Governing the Ungovernable: Leveraging the Cloud to Secure the Cloud
    🎤 Chris Farris, AWS Security Hero & Owner @ PrimeHarbor Technologies

    The cloud has democratised IT, taking it from the hands of sysadmin gatekeepers and allowing anyone in your organisation to create and deploy applications, often without the security controls you’d want. With the fall of the IT Empire, how does cybersecurity protect our assets from the roving bands of developers and DevOps, who expose things to the internet in new and unexpected ways?

    Cloud providers offer us some tools, but they fall short of actual solutions. In this session, Chris will walk you through the concept of Security Invariants, how they can be applied to your organisation, and how we can leverage event-driven architecture to cover the edge cases the cloud providers don’t account for. Whether you’re a large, well-funded enterprise or a small, scrappy start-up, you’ll get some actionable ideas (and even a bit of code) you can use to govern your cloud estate better.

    20:20 · Food, drinks & networking 🍕🍻

    ***

    🤝 Sponsors: MC Digital · synvert xgeeks

    🌐 Community Partners: DevOps Porto · Cloud Native Porto

    Thank you to MC Digital for opening the doors of the Tech Hub, to synvert xgeeks for the ongoing support, and to DevOps Porto and Cloud Native Porto for joining us.

    Spot secured = future self grateful. See you on November 4th!
